Our Story & Heritage

Textile Mastery Spanning From 1986 to Today

Beginning its journey in 1986 with wholesale handkerchief manufacturing, our company bridges Denizli's ancient textile capital heritage with contemporary global markets.

Focusing intensively on kitchen napkins and terry toweling since 2000, Nilteks has expanded its footprint worldwide through our trusted Nilteks and Nil Home brands.

By utilizing premium bamboo, Tencel™, ring spun, and open-end cotton yarns on modern jacquard and dobby looms with seasonal embroidery releases, we stand as a dependable OEM/ODM manufacturing partner for retailers, distributors, and hospitality brands across the globe.
